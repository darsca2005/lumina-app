# What users of the original hate and want

7 reviews from 3 sources (app-store, appbrain, google-play). 7 had star ratings.

Small sample. Under 30 reviews supports a direction, not a ranking. Collect more before you bet the roadmap on it.

## What they hate

| rank | theme | score | reviews | share | avg stars | sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Price and paywalls | 3.0 | 4 | 57% | 2.2 | 3 |
| 2 | Sync and lost data (thin) | 0.6 | 1 | 14% | 3.0 | 1 |
| 3 | A redesign or update made it worse (thin) | 0.6 | 1 | 14% | 3.0 | 1 |

**Price and paywalls**
- "The subscription cost is not worth it for a solo reader." (google-play, 2*) https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vikstudios.sophia
- "You cannot read more than one daily module without a paid subscription upgrade to premium." (google-play, 2*) https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vikstudios.sophia
- "Great concept for daily general knowledge, but the paywall hits right after the first sample lesson and the subscription price is too expensive if you just want to study with friends." (app-store, 2*) https://apps.apple.com/it/app/sophia-cultura-generale/id6760919622

**Sync and lost data**
- "Would love offline reading mode and a web version or desktop app so I can read courses on my laptop without losing my sync." (google-play, 3*) https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vikstudios.sophia

**A redesign or update made it worse**
- "Some localized Italian and Romanian texts had awkward machine translation phrasing before the recent update." (app-store, 3*) https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sophia-general-knowledge/id6760919622
